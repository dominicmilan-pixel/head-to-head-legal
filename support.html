Head to Head Support

Head to Head is a pass-the-phone party game from Olive Dating LLC. One person holds the phone up. Everyone else guesses the picture.

Playing

  1. Open the app. No account or sign-in is required.
  2. Choose a deck, or choose Completely Random.
  3. Wait for the 3, 2, 1 countdown.
  4. Hold the phone so the group can see the card.
  5. Tap Done for another card, or go back to the decks.

All six launch decks are free. The app does not sell decks and does not create player accounts.

Offline play

The six sample decks are included in the app and can be played with no internet connection.

If a deck has been published and this phone has finished downloading it, that downloaded copy also plays offline. Pull down on the deck shelf while online to check for a newly published deck. A download that does not finish leaves the sample deck in place.

Sounds

Countdown sounds are on by default. Turn them off in Settings. That choice is saved on the phone.

Troubleshooting

  • If a picture does not appear, force-quit the app and open it again. Sample pictures are in the app. A published picture that has already downloaded is on the phone as well.
  • If a newly published deck still shows the sample art, connect to the internet and pull down on the deck shelf.
  • If the countdown is silent, open Settings and turn Countdown Sounds on. The phone’s mute switch can also silence the game.
